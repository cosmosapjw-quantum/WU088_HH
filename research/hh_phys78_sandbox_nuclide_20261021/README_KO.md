## Meaning

The model is non-tilted LRS Bianchi I with anisotropic collisionless nonbaryonic matter and free radiation, plus isotropic reacting H1/D/T/He3/He4/Li6/Li7 gas. Every q=0..Z charge slot, Li IV, neutron, and derived free electron is represented. Gas thermal energy, ionization binding energy, electron density, gray photon absorption/emission and the mean expansion are coupled. Gas anisotropic stress is zero by its isotropic closure. The nonzero matter anisotropic stress belongs to collisionless nonbaryonic packets.

It is a new exploratory gray PI+RR model. It is not the old HH1231 collisionless, REI729 warm or HH74control63 campaign, and does not replace PR128. The finite registry is complete for the chosen primordial isotopes; full nuclear, molecular, collisional-ionization, charge-exchange and spectrally/angularly resolved reactive transport remain separate open research work. n and T start at0.

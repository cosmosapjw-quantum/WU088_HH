For a *signed integrated total thermal effect per HD molecule*, \(\Delta\epsilon_{\rm HD}\) [erg/HD], the added gas term per H is \(f_{\rm HD}\Delta\epsilon_{\rm HD}\).

\[
|\Delta \epsilon_{\rm HD}| \ \ge\ 0.01\,|\Delta e_{\rm REI}/N_{\rm H}|/f_{\rm HD}.

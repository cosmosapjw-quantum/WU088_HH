## 3. What qualifies as a source row

The recovered objects support different claims and should have separate record types.

| Record type | What is now established | What remains separate |
|---|---|---|
| `USER_TARGET_DECLARATION` | Actual original PNG, right-hand target IDs/equations, reversible flags and preserved image identity | Coefficients, cross sections, isotope/isomer/ionic state, branch probabilities, Q and physical activation |
| `DECLARED_CONDITIONAL_MODEL_ROW` | An actual row of the pinned 18-family project contract, with its literal chosen parameter, unit, provider label, model and assumptions | Independent recovery/verification of the cited primary supplier row; empirical source uncertainty and domain; physical admission |
| `PRIMARY_PROVIDER_ROW_RECOVERED` | Exact retrieved primary table/record or evaluated data section, bound to source/version/row/hash and native semantics | Model adequacy, normalization, charge-state/bare conversion and named consumer admission |
| `REUSED_DERIVED_RESULT` | An existing completed result with the same source/contract identity, for example the R1 cold kinematic record | A new measured coefficient or a fresh rate/Q calculation |

Rows from the 18-family contract are real project source records and can be registered under that exact identity. They must not be relabelled as independently recovered experimental/evaluated data merely because they carry a string such as `NACREII_section3.1` or `JENDL5_He3_MT102`.

In particular, the contract alone supplies neither the primary NACRE/Viviani/Dubovichenko table bytes nor a full JENDL evaluated reaction record. Its `EVALUATED_DECAY_INPUTS.json` reference is a pointer until that companion file and its provenance are recovered. Preserve the original JSON bytes and the literal coefficient fields; do not turn absence of covariance/error bars into zero uncertainty.

Positive supplier-intake gates are: retrieve the linked primary packet where accessible; preserve exact source identity and the actual row/section; bind the coefficient/cross-section/decay quantity, units and domain; distinguish a quoted constant from an extrapolated rate model; record branch/state/isotope and uncertainty qualifications explicitly. When only the conditional contract is recovered, retain that real but narrower record class and an explicit unresolved primary-source link.

## 4. Conditional assumptions that must travel with the 18-family rows

The contract labels its own mode `instantaneously_thermalized_all_bare_nuclei_passive_reservoir_sensitivity` and limits its admission to that conditional sensitivity. Its assumptions include:

- All eight listed isotope nuclei enter a **bare** reaction kernel; saved atomic ion fractions do not select the reactants. This does not describe the image's neutral/singly/doubly ionized material populations.
- Reacting nuclei and all produced nuclei/neutrons are instantaneously thermalized at the gas temperature. That is a chosen sensitivity model, not an established stopping/transport regime.
- The background is an inherited set of 82 saved inputs. The contract requests no new background IVPs. Its presence is not an execution or completion receipt for a new sweep.
- Constant-S and 1/v laws continue selected coefficients according to specified model forms. Native experimental/evaluated support and model continuation remain distinct.
- Binary inverse rates use a particular detailed-balance expression and symmetry/degeneracy/mass convention. Original user arrows alone do not establish those assumptions.
- Photonuclear inverse rates are set to zero for the prescribed external 5–60 eV field and absence of hard-photon reprocessing. This is not a globally zero photonuclear cross section or a thermal reverse rate at gas temperature.
- Capture photons escape under the chosen radiation closure. This is not a universal physical photon-deposition or cascade qualification.
- Li nuclear masses are constructed by subtracting three electron masses from neutral-atom masses while explicitly neglecting atomic binding. That approximation must not become an exact bare mass/Q convention in the source registry.
- The Li6 neutron row uses rounded total absorption assigned to the dominant n-alpha branch. The contract explicitly says this is a branch approximation, not an exact evaluated partial cross section.
- No immediate heating, atomic-ion history or Einstein feedback is asserted. The missing neutrino/kinetic partition remains explicit.

The Y=n/nB normalization, binary factorial convention, proper-time event density and derivative clock are part of the conditional rate definition. Their interface must be bound explicitly to the frozen compiler's reference-density normalization before any native rate is connected. A compatible dimension alone does not establish that binding.

The contract's checks, tolerances and sweep limits are historical contract fields. They are not new PASS receipts or refreshed R2 computation allowances.
